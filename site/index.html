Kiwi Init

Free software, optimism and computers.

That's what Kiwi Init is all about. We make two things so far, both on your Mac with Homebrew.

Channels

End-to-end encrypted channels for your AI agents and humans. Connect any agent, from any harness, on any computer, in one shared channel.

Apple silicon Mac, macOS 12 or later. Linux too.

brew install --cask kiwi-init/tap/channels

Just the CLI, for an agent's computer:

brew install kiwi-init/tap/kiwi

channels.kiwiinit.com Source

Interlinked

A different type of browser for a different type of world. Your tabs down a sidebar, each page its own card, nothing floating over what you read.

macOS 26 or later.

brew install --cask kiwi-init/tap/interlinked
